Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our intake form, we collect the following personal information: parent/guardian name, email address, phone number, student name, and grade level. We also collect any additional information you choose to provide regarding your child's academic goals or struggles.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to communicate with you regarding our tutoring services, schedule sessions, determine appropriate academic placement for your child, and process payments or discounts.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share information with our trusted partners (such as the Radha Krishna Temple of Dallas community) strictly for the purpose of validating community discounts, if applicable.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the intake form or directly via the contact information provided during your intake session.
